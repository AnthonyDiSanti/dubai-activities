## Maintenance rule

When a fact changes, update the card and this ledger together. A dated activity must not precede `TRIP_START_DATE_KEY`; a closed, sold-out-without-a-future-date, or pre-trip one-off should leave active data and have its selected gallery rows/files removed in lockstep. A merely tried place or firsthand rejection leaves active data but keeps its archive detail record, sourced gallery, canonical manifest rows, and attribution rows. Preserve user decisions in `src/data/archive.ts`, and do not turn an expired one-off into a recurring activity without a new source.
